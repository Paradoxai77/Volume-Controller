Gesture Control Guide

Operate hands within camera view with good lighting.

Thumb & Index Pinch

Keep index finger pointing up and pinch/spread thumb to smoothly slide volume between 0% and 100%.

Closed Fist (Hold 1s)

Fold all fingers into a closed fist to instantly toggle Mute / Unmute state.

Open Palm (Hold 1s)

Extend all 5 fingers straight to trigger Media Play / Pause on the audio stream.

Desktop Windows App
