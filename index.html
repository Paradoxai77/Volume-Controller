Gesture Control Guide

Operate hands within camera view with adequate lighting.

1. Volume Control (Pinch)

Keep your Index finger up and curl the other 3 fingers down. Move your Thumb closer or farther to smoothly slide the volume up and down.

2. Closed Fist (Mute)

Close all fingers into a tight fist and hold for 1 second until the confirmation bar fills to toggle Mute / Unmute.

3. Open Palm (Play/Pause)

Spread all 5 fingers straight facing the camera and hold for 1 second until confirmed to toggle sound playback.
