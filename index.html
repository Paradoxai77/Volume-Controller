How Gesture Recognition Works

Operate your hand within camera view:

1. Volume Control (Pinch)

Spread or pinch your Thumb and Index finger to slide the volume between 0% and 100%.

2. Closed Fist (Mute)

Make a closed fist and hold for 0.8s until confirmed to toggle Mute / Unmute.

3. Open Palm (Play/Pause)

Show an open flat hand (5 straight fingers) and hold for 0.8s to toggle audio playback.

Connect Laptop Master Audio

To control your laptop's actual Windows master volume (Spotify, YouTube, games), run python bridge.py on your PC. It links your webcam gestures directly to Windows system audio.

Windows Desktop App (OpenCV)

To run the standalone desktop version without browser, turn off Smart App Control in Windows Security and run python main.py.
